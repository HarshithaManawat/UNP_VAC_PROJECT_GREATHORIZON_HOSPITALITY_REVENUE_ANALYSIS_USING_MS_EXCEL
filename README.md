# Business Statistics with AI – Data Analysis Project

 I worked on a project titled **“Business Statistics with AI – Hospitality Revenue Management and Data Analysis”**, using Microsoft Excel to analyze business data, apply statistical techniques, and generate meaningful insights for decision-making.

The project focused on analyzing hospitality revenue data for *GrandHorizon Hotels & Resorts Ltd.*, with the objective of understanding revenue performance, room pricing, hotel classifications, destination types, and other factors influencing business profitability.

 1. Data Preprocessing and Cleaning

I began by examining the raw dataset and understanding the data dictionary and business objectives. I performed data preprocessing by identifying and removing duplicate records, handling missing values, correcting errors and spelling mistakes, standardizing inconsistent categorical values, and detecting and capping outliers. These steps helped improve data quality, consistency, and reliability for further analysis.

 2. Dashboard Creation and Data Visualization

After cleaning the data, I developed an interactive-style analytical dashboard in Excel using various charts and pivot tables to compare variables and identify trends and patterns. The visualizations included line charts, clustered column charts, 100% stacked bar charts, and other relevant charts. I analyzed key performance indicators such as Revenue per Available Room (RevPAR), Gross Operating Profit, hotel classifications, destination types, and financial quarters to understand differences in business performance.

 3. Hypothesis Testing

I applied statistical hypothesis testing to evaluate business questions and determine whether observed differences were statistically significant. The project included t-test analysis, including paired-sample comparison, and one-way ANOVA. I formulated null and alternative hypotheses, evaluated p-values, and used the results to make decisions about whether to reject or fail to reject the null hypothesis. These techniques helped assess differences in pricing and performance across the data.

 4. Correlation Analysis

I performed correlation analysis to study the relationships between important business variables, including digital advertising expenditure, active corporate accounts, aviation passenger inflow, city MICE event scores, gross operating profit, and RevPAR. This helped me understand the direction and strength of relationships between variables and identify factors associated with hospitality revenue performance.

5. Regression Analysis

I also performed regression analysis to understand how selected business variables relate to revenue performance and to evaluate the predictive relationship between variables. I interpreted the regression output, including R-squared and other statistical measures, to assess how well the model explained variation in the dependent variable.

Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I gained practical experience in data cleaning, data visualization, dashboard development, pivot tables, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, correlation, and regression using Microsoft Excel. I also learned how AI-assisted analysis can support the data analysis workflow, help structure statistical tasks, and translate numerical results into meaningful business insights.

Overall, this project strengthened my analytical thinking, statistical knowledge, Excel skills, and ability to use data-driven approaches to understand business problems and support decision-making.
